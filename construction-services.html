Construction Services

From industrial foundations to full commercial builds, Trexon delivers the complete scope of civil, electrical, and mechanical works — executed safely, on schedule, and to international quality standards.

WHAT WE DELIVER

Three Disciplines. One Standard of Excellence.

Civil Works

The company is equipped to undertake the entire scope of civil work, executing job orders for industrial foundations and slabs, culverts, acid, pipe, and cable trenches. Further, we engage in projects for commercial and industrial buildings, office premises, sports complexes, clubs, hospitals, housing compounds or villas, storage tanks, curb stones, all types of fences, interior and exterior decorative painting, as well as finishing job orders.

Electrical Works

Over the last decade, our expert electrical team has engineered, procured, installed, and commissioned large, medium, and short-range plants and projects. We achieved an outstanding status through our professional commitment, time management, and adherence to quality and international standards — from planning right through to execution. We deliver electrical projects on time, keeping our expertise in pace with our customers' needs, value, and satisfaction.

Mechanical Works

We have a long history of mechanical jobs present in every undertaking. Building on the expertise of our personnel, we deliver the highly skilled resources needed to successfully undertake projects. Competent project management superintendents, frontline supervision, and skilled craftsmen ensure each job is completed safely and on schedule. We are capable of accommodating your requirements in all mechanical aspects.
